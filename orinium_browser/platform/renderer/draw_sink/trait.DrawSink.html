Expand description

A destination for one window’s draw commands.

Callers produce commands with DrawCommand and hand them over; the sink decides when and how they reach a display.

upload only records, and present submits, so a caller may feed several command sets before showing anything — or record without ever presenting, which is what the offscreen sinks do.
