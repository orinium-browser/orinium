Converts a standalone SVG document into DrawCommand::Fill commands that the existing wgpu DrawCommand::Fill pipeline can render directly, instead of software-rasterizing to pixels (rasterize). Paths are expressed in viewport pixel space (the same space rasterize fills into), so no extra viewBox transform is needed: the caller positions the resulting commands at the SVG’s content box.

Only solid fills and strokes are emitted, matching the standalone rasterizer scope (gradients fall back to their last stop’s color, <clipPath> is not emitted as a clip command). Gradients and clip paths are intentionally out of scope for this conversion, mirroring rasterize.
