Expand description

A transport’s consuming half.

There is no is_disconnected here on purpose: a poll that finds an empty queue cannot tell “nothing yet” from “nothing ever”, and answering wrongly either way would let a caller discard live work. A consumer learns the channel is closed from Consume::recv returning Disconnected.
